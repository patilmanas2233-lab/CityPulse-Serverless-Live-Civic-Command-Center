Frequently Asked Questions

Can I submit reports anonymously?

Yes. You can opt out of contact sharing while still tracking the progress of your submitted ticket through its unique ID.

What types of issues should I report here?

CityPulse is designed for non-emergency issues like potholes, broken streetlights, graffiti, illegal dumping, and damaged sidewalks. For immediate emergencies, please contact 911 or local emergency lines.

How long does typical resolution take?

Resolution windows vary by hazard severity and department availability. Average non-critical issues are acknowledged within 24 hours.
